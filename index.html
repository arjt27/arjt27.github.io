About Rainbow and Bites

Welcome to Rainbow and Bites—a single-screen browser toy for a quick break. Check New York, Chicago, and Los Angeles for local time skies and simulated weather conditions alongside rooftops, building silhouettes, a drifting plane, and trees along the hills.

In Move mode, drag directly on the rainbow or hills to reposition them, or switch to Line, Oval, or Box mode to sketch shapes across the skyscape before tapping New Rainbow or Random Snack.

Pi Sign-In: optional sign-in via Pi Browser for a personal greeting. Sandbox mode is hardcoded to true and remains locked ON for safe testing.
