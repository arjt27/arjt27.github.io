About Rainbow and Bites

Welcome to Rainbow and Bites—a calm, single-screen browser toy made for a quiet pause between clicks. Switch between New York, Chicago, and Los Angeles to see live weather conditions and temperatures, shift the sky mood, draw a fresh rainbow, and roll a random snack idea.

There are no timers, leaderboards, or complicated rules here—just a cozy spot to make a rainbow, check skies across the US, and grab a snack idea.

If you enjoy the vibe here, thanks for stopping by, sharing the page, or checking out more cozy games, posts, and creative projects.
