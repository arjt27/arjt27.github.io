About Rainbow and Bites

Welcome to Rainbow and Bites—a calm, single-screen browser toy for a quick breather between clicks. Check New York, Chicago, and Los Angeles for live weather conditions and temperatures alongside local time skies, complete with rooftops, building silhouettes, a drifting plane, and trees along the hills. Drag right on the rainbow or hills to move them, or switch to Line, Oval, or Box mode to sketch your own lines and shapes across the skyscape before tapping for a fresh snack idea. There are no timers or complicated rules here—just a cozy spot to paint the sky, play with the scene, and grab a bite.
