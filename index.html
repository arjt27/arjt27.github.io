Rainbow and Bites

Welcome to Rainbow and Bites—a calm, single-screen browser toy made to give you a quiet pause between clicks. Every tap or city switch pulls current weather from New York, London, Tokyo, Paris, and Sydney, shifting the sky mood, drawing a fresh rainbow with subtle variations in arc and color palette, and serving up a quick random snack idea.

Bringing this project into the Pi ecosystem is super exciting, and it feels great to share something simple, lightweight, and fun with Pioneers exploring the Pi Browser. There are no timers, leaderboards, or complicated rules here—just a cozy spot to make a rainbow, check skies around the globe, and grab a snack idea.

If you enjoy the vibe here, I would love your support—whether that means tapping through a few rainbows, sharing the page with friends, or exploring the rest of the site for more cozy games, posts, and creative projects.
