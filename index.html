About Rainbow and Bites

Welcome to Rainbow and Bites—a calm, single-screen browser toy for a quick breather between clicks. Check New York, Chicago, and Los Angeles for live weather conditions and temperatures alongside local time skies, complete with a drifting plane and trees along the hills. Drag right on the rainbow or hills to move and shape them as you like, and tap for a fresh snack idea whenever you want one. There are no timers or complicated rules here—just a cozy spot to play with the sky and grab a bite.
