About Rainbow and Bites

Welcome to Rainbow and Bites—a calm, single-screen browser toy made for a quiet pause between clicks. Check today's Sky & Bite or switch between New York, Chicago, and Los Angeles to see live weather conditions and temperatures, local time skies, a plane and trees, drag-to-move rainbows and hills, and a random snack idea.

There are no timers, leaderboards, or complicated rules here—just a cozy spot to make a rainbow, check skies across the US, and grab a snack idea.
