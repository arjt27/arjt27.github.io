Rainbow and Bites

Welcome to Rainbow and Bites—a calm, single-screen browser toy made to give you a quiet pause between clicks. Every tap or city switch pulls current weather from New York, Chicago, and Los Angeles, showing live conditions and temperatures, shifting the sky mood, and drawing a fresh rainbow with subtle variations in arc and color palette—while you can roll a highly random snack idea whenever you want one.

There are no timers, leaderboards, or complicated rules here—just a cozy spot to make a rainbow, check skies across the US, and grab a snack idea.

If you enjoy the vibe here, I would love your support—whether that means tapping through a few rainbows, sharing the page with friends, or exploring the rest of the site for more cozy games, posts, and creative projects.
