Privacy Policy

App: Rainbow and Bites (https://app.funvideogames.biz)

Rainbow and Bites is a client-side browser toy operated as an independent creator project. It does not require an account to browse skyscapes or snack ideas.

What Happens in Your Browser

  • Pi Sign-In: If you choose to sign in via the Pi SDK inside the Pi Browser, your Pi username is used locally in the session for a personal greeting.
  • Weather & Sketches: Weather lookups are requested from Open-Meteo for the selected city, and skyscape drawings or snack selections run locally in your browser.
  • Manual Tip Jar: A public Pi wallet address (GCM47GS76HRSNB55U4W4S5VDGBRSMEUOGYLOBRTT5KRAWW5YEI5YQ6TN) is provided if you choose to send a manual tip from your Pi Wallet.
